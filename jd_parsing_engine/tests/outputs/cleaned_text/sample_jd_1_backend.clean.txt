Senior Backend Engineer

Role Overview
We are looking for a Senior Backend Engineer to join our platform team and help
scale our payments infrastructure to millions of users.

Responsibilities
- Design and build REST APIs consumed by internal and external clients
- Own services written in Python and Go running on AWS and Kubernetes
- Mentor junior engineers and participate in code reviews
- Collaborate with product and design to ship features end to end

Requirements
- 5+ years of professional software engineering experience
- Strong proficiency in Python and SQL
- Experience with Docker, Kubernetes, and CI/CD pipelines
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related field
- Excellent communication skills

Preferred Qualifications
- Experience with Kafka or other streaming systems
- Familiarity with Terraform / infrastructure as code
- Prior experience at a fintech company

Benefits
We offer competitive compensation, remote flexibility, and a generous learning
stipend.
